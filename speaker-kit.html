Speaker bio

Use this short bio when introducing Harish at your event.

Harish Reddy Duggempudi is a software developer and community builder based in Hyderabad, India. He builds web products and explores AI and cloud technologies. He founded Student Nexus to help students discover developer tools, learning opportunities, and mentors, and serves as President of Innovista Hub at Malla Reddy College of Engineering. As a Senior Microsoft Learn Student Ambassador and Lead Organizer with the Microsoft Fabric User Group Hyderabad, he contributes to student and developer communities through workshops, hackathons, and technical sessions.
